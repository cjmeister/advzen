E-MTB Tours

Quiet trails. Long distances. Freedom on two wheels.

Our E-MTB tours offer an easy, independent way to explore the Algarve — long climbs made effortless, flowy descents, quiet backroads, and the freedom to ride entirely at your own pace. You choose the route, we supply the track, drop you at the start point, and pick you up when the adventure ends.

Each ride is self-guided: you’ll follow a GPX track on your phone, moving through forests, hills, villages, and hidden viewpoints along the way. Tracks can be tailored to your skill level — from easy scenic loops to more challenging off-road routes — depending on your mountain biking experience and confidence.

We design routes for all styles: road-friendly loops, classic MTB terrain, and tougher enduro-style trails for riders looking for something wilder. We’ll transport you and the bike to the start of the trail, then meet you at the finish. It’s flexible, simple, and a great way to see the Algarve without needing a guide.

Adventure as Therapy — one pedal stroke at a time.

What’s Included

  • Self-guided E-MTB tour
  • Tailored GPX track (easy → challenging)
