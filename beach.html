Beach Days

Warm sand. Clear water. Easy escape.

The Algarve’s beaches are some of the best in Europe — wide open sands, quiet coves, island shores, and long shallow waters perfect for swimming or wandering. At ADV ZEN, we make it easy for you to enjoy them without the stress of peak-season traffic or impossible parking.

We offer beach drop-off and pick-up so you can spend the day by the ocean without worrying about the crowds. Simply choose the beach you want to visit, and we’ll get you there — leaving you free to swim, nap, read, explore, or just disappear into the horizon for a few hours.

If you prefer to go on your own, you’re welcome to drive or cycle — the beaches are close, and we’ll happily point you toward our favourite spots.

Adventure as Therapy — sometimes all you need is the sea.

What’s Included

  • Drop-off and pick-up to selected Algarve beaches
  • Choice of island beaches, quiet coves, or wide open sands
  • Flexible schedule depending on tides and weather
  • Recommendations for the best spots each day
  • Easy, stress-free way to enjoy the coast during busy months

Who It’s For

Anyone who wants a slow, simple day by the ocean — swimmers, wanderers, readers, sunseekers, families, couples, or riders taking a break between big adventure days. Perfect for anyone wanting an effortless escape to the sea.
