Stand-Up Paddleboarding

The Algarve’s water is as much a part of the adventure as the trails. From still inland reservoirs to the quiet channels of the Ria Formosa and the open sea, stand-up paddleboarding is a calm way to stay connected to the natural landscape.

We’ll take care of everything you need: the board, paddle, pump, and transport to your chosen spot. You just bring your balance and a sense of curiosity. From sunrise paddles to peaceful afternoons on still water, every session offers a quiet reset — a moment to breathe and drift.

Adventure as Therapy — this time on water.

What’s Included

  • Stand-up paddleboard, paddle, and pump
  • Solo sessions or guided lessons
  • Transfer to reservoirs or beaches
  • Optional lagoon tour for longer explorations
  • Calm, scenic locations chosen for the best experience

Who It’s For

Anyone who wants gentle adventure in nature — from beginners learning to stand and paddle, to experienced paddlers looking for new places to explore. Perfect for those seeking calm, balance, and time on the water.

Pricing

Board rental: €15 per hour
Includes board, paddle, and pump for self-guided paddling. Transfers are not included — drop-off and pick-up can be arranged for an additional fee.

Guided paddleboarding: €25 per hour
Includes instruction, support, and transport to and from the paddle location.

Equipment must be returned in good condition. Damage may incur additional costs.
