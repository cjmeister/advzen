Massage & Body Recovery

Slow down. Reset. Let the body breathe again.

Long days on the bike, on the water, or on the trails deserve proper recovery. Our Massage & Body Recovery sessions are designed to help you unwind deeply — releasing tension, easing tired muscles, and bringing balance back after your adventures in the Algarve.

A trained massage therapist comes directly to the property for a private, quiet, tailored session. Whether you need a sports massage to work through stiffness or a slower, restorative treatment to calm the nervous system, each session is adapted to your needs and the activities you’ve been doing.

These sessions are simple but effective — a chance to breathe, reset, and feel grounded again before your next ride or explore day.

Adventure as Therapy — recovery included.

What’s Included

  • Private, on-property massage session
  • Choice of sports or restorative massage
  • Adapted to your needs and recent activities
  • Calm, quiet setting for full relaxation
  • Available to all guests staying at ADV ZEN

Pricing

Massage session: €80 per hour
One-on-one, private, on-site massage by a professional therapist.

Sessions must be booked at least one day in advance to ensure availability.

Who It’s For

Anyone needing recovery after riding, paddling, hiking, or training — or simply those wanting a peaceful hour to themselves. Ideal for restoring movement, reducing tension, and supporting the body during active days at ADV ZEN.
