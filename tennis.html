Play Tennis at ADVZEN

Quiet court. Open skies. Your game, your rhythm.

Our private tennis court sits in the middle of the Algarve countryside — peaceful, open, and surrounded by orange trees and warm light. It’s a calm place to train, play, or unwind between days of riding and adventure.

Whether you’re practicing solo or playing with friends, the court is yours to enjoy. Rent it for an hour, a morning, or a full day, and make the most of the quiet atmosphere. A ball machine is available for anyone who wants focused practice or a good workout without a partner.

Play at your own pace, surrounded by nature. Stay as long as you like. This isn’t a crowded club — it’s a relaxed space where the sound of the ball echoes into open air.

Adventure as Therapy — sometimes it’s a perfect rally.
