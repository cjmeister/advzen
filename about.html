At ADV ZEN, we believe in the kind of adventure that gives you space — to move, to breathe, to reset.

We’re based in a quiet Portuguese village near Moncarapacho, between the Algarve’s mountains and the sea. Here, we welcome people who want something simple: time outdoors, on two wheels or two feet, away from the noise.

Some come for the ocean and the warmth. Some for the rides and the adventure. Others just to slow down and breathe. Many for all of it.

You can stay with us or just drop in for a day. Go for a guided motorbike ride, take out an e-mountain bike, paddle along the coast, stretch out in the sun — or do nothing at all. No pressure. No fixed plan.

ADV ZEN isn’t a resort or a retreat. It’s a calm, down-to-earth base for people who find peace in motion and relax in nature.
