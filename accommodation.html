Our Accommodation

The main accommodation is a traditional two-bedroom Portuguese house — simple, quiet, and extremely comfortable. It’s well-equipped with everything you need to settle in, whether you’re here for a weekend escape or a longer stay.

The house sleeps up to six people comfortably, and can stretch to eight if needed:

  • One bedroom has a queen-size bed and an extra single bed — ideal for small families or friends sharing.
  • The second bedroom has a large double bed.
  • The living room can be turned into a sleeping space too, with fold-out beds or mattresses for larger groups.

Inside, there’s a fully equipped kitchen, hot water, a cosy lounge, and a cinema/TV room for relaxing after a day out. There’s also 10 Gbps fibre internet — fast enough for remote work, streaming, or syncing footage from your rides.

Outside, you’ve got access to the wider property: a pool, a private tennis court, a fire pit, and a large BBQ and outdoor living area — perfect for shared meals, slow evenings, and good company.

We’re also expanding — glamping-style accommodation is planned for 2027, offering a more open and nature-connected way to stay.

Whether you’re travelling solo, with friends, or as a family, this space offers comfort, calm, and easy access to everything that surrounds it.
