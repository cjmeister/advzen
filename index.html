ADV ZEN — Adventure as Therapy.

Located between the Algarve’s mountains and beaches — just 25 minutes from Faro Airport — ADV Zen is a peaceful base for both adventure and rest.

Whether you stay for a few days or just join us for a session, there’s plenty to do — or not do. From enduro and adventure motorbiking to e-mountain biking, stand-up paddleboarding, yoga, hiking, kitesurfing and more.

We see adventure as therapy. That’s the idea behind ADV ZEN.

Motorbike Tours Across the Algarve and Beyond

At the core of ADV ZEN are our adventure and enduro motorbike tours. Whether you’re riding deep into the hills on technical singletrack or covering long distances on a mix of road and trail, these tours are built to challenge, clear your head, and show you parts of Portugal most people never see. We run everything from single-day enduro rides to multi-day adventure tours that span the country. Bring your own bike or ride one of ours — we’ll guide the way.
