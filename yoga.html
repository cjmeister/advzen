Yoga at ADVZEN

Five days of movement, stillness, and Algarve calm.

From May to September, ADV ZEN becomes a space for immersive outdoor yoga workshops — slow mornings, open-air practice, warm sunsets, and long breaks to enjoy the beaches and countryside around us. Each workshop runs for five days, led by an experienced yoga practitioner who brings their own style, flow, and energy to the week.

Every day includes two yoga sessions: a gentle, grounding practice in the morning and a restorative or slow flow session in the late afternoon. The middle of the day is yours — spend it at the beach, explore the local villages, rest by the pool, or simply enjoy the quiet of the property.

Each workshop includes breakfast and dinner. Breakfast is served at the lodge, and dinner is enjoyed at one of the nearby restaurants, giving you a taste of the Algarve’s relaxed food culture.

Workshops vary in theme, focus, and teaching style. Every instructor brings something unique — from breathwork to mobility, meditation, strength, or deep recovery.

Adventure as Therapy — the softer side of the journey.

What’s Included

  • Five-day outdoor yoga workshop
  • Two guided sessions per day (morning and late afternoon)
  • Led by experienced yoga practitioners
  • Breakfast included each morning
  • Dinner included each evening (local restaurants)
  • Free time to explore beaches, trails, and the Algarve
  • Calm open-air practice spaces around the property

Pricing

Workshop prices vary depending on the teacher, program, group size, and inclusions. Full details and pricing are posted for each workshop as they become available.

Who It’s For

Anyone seeking a deeper reset — from beginners wanting structure and guidance to experienced yogis looking for a peaceful, nature-rich retreat. Ideal for combining movement, calm, and slow days under the Algarve sun.
