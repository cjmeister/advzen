Hiking

Quiet trails. Open views. Slow steps through the Algarve.

The Algarve is filled with hidden ridgelines, forest paths, coastal cliffs, and quiet countryside tracks waiting to be explored. At ADV ZEN, hiking is a simple, peaceful way to see the landscape — at your pace, in your own rhythm.

If you want to explore further afield, we can drop you at the start of a trail and pick you up when you’re done. It’s an easy, flexible way to enjoy the best hiking spots without worrying about logistics. We can also provide free GPX routes or point you to our favourite tracks around the mountains, valleys, and coastline.

If you’d rather go independently, you’re welcome to walk straight from the property — there are plenty of beautiful trails you can follow without any assistance.

No rush, no pressure — just movement, nature, and open air.

Adventure as Therapy — one step at a time.

What’s Included

  • Access to local hiking trails around the property
  • Free GPX tracks for popular routes
  • Recommendations for the best hikes (difficulty, distance, scenery)
  • Optional drop-off and pick-up service for trails further away
  • Flexible timing depending on where you want to explore

Who It’s For

Anyone who enjoys slow exploration — from gentle nature walks to longer half-day hikes. Ideal for guests who want quiet time between big adventure days or simply want to see the Algarve from a different angle.
