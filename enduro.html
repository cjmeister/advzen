Enduro Bike Tours

Smaller bikes. Tougher terrain. Pure exploration.

Leave the map behind and dive into the real Algarve — single tracks, rocky climbs, forest trails, riverbeds, and whatever else the day brings. Our Enduro Bike Tours are for riders who love discovery, challenge, and the thrill of riding into the unknown.

These rides are free, informal, and completely unplanned. We pick a destination, point the bikes toward it, and follow whatever tracks we find along the way. Expect steep sections, loose rocks, technical climbs, and narrow trails that twist through the hills. It’s not about speed — it’s about exploring, problem-solving, and enjoying every obstacle the landscape throws at us.

You’ll be riding smaller enduro bikes (your own or a rental), built for agility and rough terrain. Most days cover around 100 km, depending on how gnarly the tracks get. The pace is always easy-going and adapted to the riders — no pressure, just good company and real adventure.

Accommodation isn’t required, but if you’re staying for multiple days, you can stay at ADV ZEN or find your own spot nearby. Bike rentals can be arranged if needed.

Adventure as Therapy — this time with dust, sweat, and a smile.

What’s Included

  • Free, informal enduro rides around the Algarve
  • Unplanned routes with natural obstacles and varied terrain
  • Guidance, group support, and help through tough sections
  • Easy-going pace matched to the riders
  • Roughly 100 km per day, depending on difficulty

Who It’s For

Riders who want raw, real off-road exploration — no schedules, no pressure, just a destination and a willingness to ride whatever comes. Perfect for those who enjoy technical terrain, tight trails, and discovering the Algarve’s hidden backcountry.
